🎯 Course Objectives

At the end of the course, students will be able to:

  1. Understand the principles, mindset, and methodologies of Design Thinking for human-centered problem solving.
  2. Observe, empathize, and analyze user needs to formulate well-defined engineering and societal problems.
  3. Apply creative and innovative thinking through systematic ideation, concept generation, and evaluation.
  4. Develop, prototype, test, and iteratively improve solutions using user feedback and appropriate tools.
  5. Identify innovation opportunities, create value propositions, understand basic IPR, and communicate solutions effectively.

🎯 Course Outcomes

  • CO1 (K2): Explain principles, processes and mindset of Design Thinking.
  • CO2 (K3): Apply empathy, observation and problem-framing techniques.
  • CO3 (K3): Apply creative and systematic ideation techniques.
  • CO4 (K4): Analyze solution concepts and prototypes using user feedback.
  • CO5 (K4): Analyze innovation potential considering value, feasibility, sustainability, societal impact and IPR.
