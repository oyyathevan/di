🎯 Unit Outcomes

Unit I — Foundations & Empathy

Students will be able to understand users, identify their needs, and translate observations into meaningful design opportunities.

Unit II — Problem Definition, Analysis & Opportunity Identification

Students will be able to analyze user research, identify root causes, frame well-defined problems, and recognize opportunities for innovative solutions.

Unit III — Creativity, Ideation & Concept Development

Students will be able to generate diverse solution concepts using creative techniques and systematically select promising ideas based on defined criteria.

Unit IV — Prototyping, Experimentation & Validation

Students will be able to develop, prototype, and iteratively improve solutions using user feedback and appropriate physical or digital tools.

Unit V — Innovation, Value Creation & Communication

Students will be able to identify innovation opportunities, create value propositions, understand basic IPR, and communicate innovative solutions effectively.
